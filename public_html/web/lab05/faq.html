SmartScrip, §coins, and you!

What is SmartScrip?

SmartScrip (§coin) is an amazing blockchain based digital currency. All corporations in Sol sector offer payment in the form of §coins, for your convenience.

How do I spend it?

You can spend your §coin just like credits, at any station that is a member of the SmartBank vendors union, or with any private individual holding a SmartScrip vendor coin in their digital wallet.

What determines the value of §coins in my wallet?

That's the most amazing thing about SmartScrip!
SmartScrip has a dynamic value, adjusted according to job performance, loyalty, health, marital status, and other important factors. Make sure to reference the employee handbook often, and the value of your § will be soaring in no time!

SmartScrip uses the blockchain, does that mean transactions are public?

Of course! SmartBank's android tellers are constantly monitoring the §ledger, making critical updates to the value of your §coins. If the ledger were private, then the value of your §coins could never go up!

What if I have other questions?

Speak to any SmartBank android to have all your questions answered, in a friendly helpful tone!
